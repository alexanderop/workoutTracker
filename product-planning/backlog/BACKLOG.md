## Story Point Reference
- **1 point**: Trivial change, < 1 hour
- **2 points**: Simple change, half day
- **3 points**: Medium complexity, 1-2 days
- **5 points**: Complex, 3-5 days
- **8 points**: Very complex, needs breakdown
- **13 points**: Epic-level, must be split

## Status Definitions
- **New**: Just added, needs refinement
- **Refinement**: Being detailed and estimated
- **Ready**: Fully refined, ready for sprint
- **In Progress**: Currently being worked on
- **Done**: Completed and verified
