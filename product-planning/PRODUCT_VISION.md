# Product Vision

## Vision Statement
<!-- One sentence describing the ultimate goal of this product -->
To empower fitness enthusiasts to track, analyze, and improve their workout performance through an intuitive, offline-capable progressive web app.

## Problem Statement
<!-- What problem are we solving? For whom? -->
Fitness enthusiasts struggle to consistently track their workouts, monitor progress over time, and make data-driven decisions about their training. Existing solutions are either too complex, require constant internet connectivity, or lack the flexibility to support different workout styles.

## Target Users
<!-- Who are we building this for? -->
- Casual gym-goers who want simple workout logging
- Serious lifters tracking progressive overload
- CrossFit/functional fitness athletes with varied workouts
- Home workout enthusiasts

## Key Value Propositions
1. **Offline-first** - Works without internet, syncs when available
2. **Flexible tracking** - Supports various workout styles and exercise types
3. **Progress visualization** - Clear insights into performance over time
4. **Template system** - Save and reuse workout structures

## Success Metrics
<!-- How do we measure success? -->
- [ ] Daily active users
- [ ] Workouts logged per user per week
- [ ] User retention at 30/60/90 days
- [ ] App store rating

## Non-Goals (Out of Scope)
<!-- What are we explicitly NOT doing? -->
- Social features / sharing
- AI-generated workout plans (for now)
- Wearable device integration (for now)
- Nutrition tracking
