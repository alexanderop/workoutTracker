# User Personas

## Persona 1: "Casual Carl"

### Demographics
- **Age**: 25-40
- **Occupation**: Office worker
- **Fitness Level**: Beginner to intermediate
- **Tech Savvy**: Moderate

### Goals
- Stay healthy and fit
- Maintain consistency with workouts
- Track progress without complexity

### Pain Points
- Forgets what weights he used last time
- Gets overwhelmed by complex fitness apps
- Doesn't want to spend time setting things up

### Behaviors
- Works out 2-3x per week
- Follows simple routines
- Prefers gym workouts
- Uses phone during rest periods

### Quote
> "I just want to know if I'm getting stronger without spending 10 minutes logging everything."

### Feature Priorities
1. Quick, simple logging
2. See last workout weights easily
3. Basic progress tracking

## Persona 2: "Serious Sarah"

### Demographics
- **Age**: 22-35
- **Occupation**: Various
- **Fitness Level**: Intermediate to advanced
- **Tech Savvy**: High

### Goals
- Progressive overload on compound lifts
- Track PRs and milestones
- Optimize training program

### Pain Points
- Needs detailed history for each exercise
- Wants to see trends over weeks/months
- Current apps don't support her specific routine structure

### Behaviors
- Works out 4-6x per week
- Follows structured programs (5/3/1, PPL, etc.)
- Tracks everything meticulously
- Analyzes her data regularly

### Quote
> "I need to see my squat progression over the last 12 weeks with volume and intensity."

### Feature Priorities
1. Detailed exercise history with graphs
2. Program/template support
3. PR tracking and notifications
4. Export data for analysis

---

## Persona 3: "Functional Fiona"

### Demographics
- **Age**: 28-45
- **Occupation**: Various
- **Fitness Level**: Intermediate
- **Tech Savvy**: Moderate to high

### Goals
- Track varied workouts (CrossFit-style)
- Log times, rounds, and benchmarks
- Compare benchmark workout times

### Pain Points
- Most apps are built for traditional gym workouts
- Needs to track time-based workouts
- Wants benchmark comparisons

### Behaviors
- Does CrossFit, F45, or similar
- Workouts vary daily
- Competes in local competitions
- Tracks benchmark WODs

### Quote
> "I need to log that I did Fran in 4:32 and see how that compares to my last attempt."

### Feature Priorities
1. Timer integration
2. Benchmark tracking
3. Flexible workout structures
4. Quick workout logging

---

## Persona 4: "Home Workout Henry"

### Demographics
- **Age**: 30-50
- **Occupation**: Remote worker / Parent
- **Fitness Level**: Beginner to intermediate
- **Tech Savvy**: Moderate

### Goals
- Stay fit without gym membership
- Work out around family schedule
- Use minimal equipment

### Pain Points
- Limited equipment options
- Needs bodyweight exercise support
- Workouts interrupted frequently

### Behaviors
- Works out at home
- Short, flexible workout windows
- Uses dumbbells, bands, bodyweight
- Often works out early morning or during lunch

### Quote
> "I've got 20 minutes and my dumbbells. What should I do today?"

### Feature Priorities
1. Bodyweight exercise support
2. Quick workout templates
3. Equipment filtering
4. Offline support (no wifi in garage)

---

## Using Personas with Claude

When working with Claude on features, reference these personas:

- "Does this solve Serious Sarah's pain point about X?"
- "Write acceptance criteria considering Functional Fiona's needs"
