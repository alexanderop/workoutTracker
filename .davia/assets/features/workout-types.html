Workout Types

Workouts are built from blocks. Each block is a different training modality with its own timing and tracking logic. 🧱

📊 Block-Based Architecture

Every workout is a sequence of blocks. You can mix and match blocks to create hybrid workouts:

💪 Strength Blocks

Best for: Traditional weightlifting with progressive overload

  • Track: Weight (kg), Reps, RIR (Reps In Reserve)
  • Example: Barbell Back Squat 3x5 @ 100kg, RIR 2
  • Use case: Compound lifts, accessory work, strength cycles

⏱️ AMRAP (As Many Rounds As Possible)

Best for: High-volume conditioning with predictable work time

  • Track: Rounds completed + partial reps
  • Example: 20min AMRAP - 5 Pull-ups, 10 Push-ups, 15 Air Squats
  • Use case: Benchmark WODs (Cindy), endurance training

🔁 EMOM (Every Minute On the Minute)

Best for: Paced intervals with built-in rest

  • Track: Completed minutes, missed minutes
  • Example: 12min EMOM - Min 1: 10 Burpees, Min 2: 15 KB Swings
  • Use case: Interval training, skill practice under fatigue

🔥 Tabata

Best for: Max effort sprint intervals

  • Track: Reps per round
  • Example: 8 rounds Tabata (20s work / 10s rest) - Air Squats
  • Use case: High-intensity finishers, single-movement capacity tests

⏲️ For Time

Best for: Speed-focused workouts with optional time cap

  • Track: Completion time, whether you beat the cap
  • Example: For Time (cap 10min) - 21-15-9 Thrusters, Pull-ups
  • Use case: Benchmark WODs (Fran, Grace), competition simulation

🎨 Example Hybrid Workout

You can combine blocks to create complex training sessions:

Monday - Upper Body + Conditioning

1. Strength Block: Bench Press 5x3

2. Strength Block: Barbell Rows 4x8

3. AMRAP Block: 15min - 10 Push-ups, 10 Ring Rows, 10 KB Swings
