Templates and Benchmarks

Two ways to save and reuse workouts, each serving a different purpose. 📚

🔄 Templates vs Benchmarks

Understanding the difference helps you organize your training effectively:

📋 Workout Templates

Purpose: Save custom workout structures for reuse

Key Characteristics

  • Fully customizable: You create and define every block
  • Editable: Can modify or delete templates anytime
  • Personal library: Build your own collection over time
  • Flexible: Load into builder and adjust before starting

Common Use Cases

  • Weekly splits: "Monday Push", "Wednesday Legs", "Friday Pull"
  • Favorite combos: Strength + conditioning pairings you like
  • Programs: Saving each week of a 12-week cycle
  • Quick starts: Pre-built workouts for busy days

Example Template

Template Name: "Heavy Upper + Short Metcon"

Block 1: Bench Press 5x3

Block 2: Weighted Pull-ups 4x5

Block 3: 10min AMRAP - 10 DB Press, 10 Rows, 10 Push-ups

🏆 Benchmark Workouts

Purpose: Track performance on standardized workouts over time

Key Characteristics

  • Pre-defined: Standard CrossFit benchmarks (Fran, Cindy, Murph, etc.)
  • Non-editable: Structure is locked to maintain consistency
  • Performance tracking: Compare results across attempts
  • Universal: Same workouts athletes worldwide use for comparison

Common Use Cases

  • Progress testing: Re-test every 8-12 weeks
  • Competition prep: Practice known benchmark formats
  • Community comparison: See how you stack up on "Fran"
  • Goal setting: Target specific benchmark times/scores

Example Benchmarks

Fran: For Time - 21-15-9 Thrusters (95lb/65lb), Pull-ups

Cindy: 20min AMRAP - 5 Pull-ups, 10 Push-ups, 15 Air Squats

Murph: For Time - 1mi Run, 100 Pull-ups, 200 Push-ups, 300 Squats, 1mi Run

📊 How Both Feed History

Completed workouts from both templates and benchmarks save to your history:

  • Templates: Track volume trends, progressive overload on custom workouts
  • Benchmarks: See time/round improvements, measure fitness gains

The app automatically tags completed workouts with their source (template or benchmark) for easy filtering.

💡 Choosing the Right Tool

  • Use Templates when you want reusable custom workouts you might tweak
  • Use Benchmarks when you want standardized performance testing
  • Use Both for a complete training approach: templates for daily work, benchmarks for testing
